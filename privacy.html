Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 20, 2026

At SuOrbit, we take user privacy seriously. Since our website is built entirely as a static portal hosted on GitHub Pages, we operate under a zero-tracking framework. We do not use cookies, run analytical trackers, or maintain backend servers to capture your IP address or query history.

1. Data Collection

We do not collect personal identifiers, email addresses (except when explicitly submitted to our newsletter form), search trends, or geographical positions. Browsing our pages is entirely anonymous.

2. Third-Party Hosting

SuOrbit is hosted on GitHub Pages. Any connection to this website is served under SSL encryption. GitHub may collect basic infrastructure logs (such as IP addresses and requests metadata) to prevent abuse and maintain server uptime. These logs are managed strictly under GitHub's Privacy Statement and are not visible to SuOrbit operators.

3. Newsletter Subscription

When you subscribe to our newsletter, you voluntarily supply your email address. We use this address solely to distribute weekly simplified news summaries. We do not sell, rent, or lease our list to external corporations. You can unsubscribe at any time by clicking the hyperlink at the bottom of our emails.

4. External Hyperlinks

Our summaries include references to the original reporting channels (e.g. Wired, Reuters, TechCrunch). We are not responsible for the privacy procedures or content standards of these external portals.

5. Revisions

We may modify this privacy document from time to time. Any modifications will be posted directly to this page with an updated timestamp.
