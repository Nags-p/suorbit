Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Complete Client-Side Generation

SuOrbit QR Studio is architected with a strict privacy-by-design model. Every QR code generated or scanned through our platform is rendered directly inside your device's browser using JavaScript and HTML5 Canvas / SVG technologies.

We do not operate any backend database or ingestion pipeline that stores your input payloads, Wi-Fi credentials, personal contact information, or scanned media.

2. No Analytical Cookies or Trackers

We do not use tracking cookies, commercial ad-trackers, or cross-site profiling scripts. Your interactions with the tool are completely confidential and unmonitored.

3. Camera Access for QR Scanning

When you choose to use the live QR Code scanner, your browser requests permission to access your device camera. Video frames captured by your camera are analyzed solely within your browser memory using the native BarcodeDetector API or local WebAssembly/JS routines. No video feed or snapshot is ever uploaded or transmitted.

4. Local Storage Library

Any QR codes saved via the "Save to Library" feature are stored strictly in your browser's local storage (`localStorage`). This data stays on your machine and can be cleared at any time with a single click of the "Clear All" button.

5. Hosting Infrastructure

This static website is deployed on GitHub Pages. Any standard connection to this domain is delivered via end-to-end SSL/TLS encryption.
